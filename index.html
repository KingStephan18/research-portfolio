01 / CYBERSECURITYEXECUTABLE DECISION LAB

Permission Boundary Lab

Turn an authorization rule into a test. Check exact scope, expiration and revocation—and keep a proposed action separate from the permission to perform it.

Python · 24 synthetic scenarios · 23 unit/CLI tests

Inspect the experiment

The boundary

The authenticated principal, grants, revocations, clock and approvals arrive separately from the request. A claimed role, an instruction in a note or an “approved” field cannot widen that authority.

What ran

The guarded decision function matched all 24 authored scenarios: four allows and 20 denials. It produced no false allows or false denials on this matrix. An intentionally incomplete action-only baseline produced 12 false allows.

What this does not establish

This is local decision logic on synthetic fixtures. It does not authenticate users, execute tools, test an AI model or secure a real account. A production executor must protect its policy context and bind authorization to the operation without a race.

02 / AI & SECURITYWORKING DEMONSTRATION

Evidence Ledger

A reference checker for claims that look better supported than they are. Inspect the source links, detect shared origins, and keep contradictory records visible.

Python · Strict input validation · 45 unit/CLI tests

Try the evidence viewer ↗
Read the study

The question

Can a small tool flag weak evidence structure without pretending to automate research judgment?

The Python sample checks that a claim’s references exist, counts declared independent origins, and flags documentary contradictions. Reconstructions are excluded from documentary support. It uses the standard library, makes no network requests and calls no model.

A deliberately difficult example

Two reports repeat one event log. The example claim says both that the job completed and that it has two independent confirmations. The checker flags the shared origin. A person must still split the compound claim and correct its wording.

What ran

45 unit and command-line tests passed under Python 3.13.5. Cases include invalid schemas, duplicates, unknown sources, reconstruction, context-only evidence, conflicts and malformed JSON. Five synthetic claims were processed to produce the bundled results.

A clean TRACEABLE state requires documentary support with no declared contradiction and no structural flag. Shared origins and reconstructions require review. The checker never assigns a truth verdict.

03 / HUMAN IDENTITYSYNTHETIC RESEARCH EXERCISE

Same name, different people

An online tree makes a confident link. A closer look at fathers, addresses and chronology tells a more complicated story. Follow the competing explanations.

Genealogy · Source correlation · Uncertainty

Read the case

All people, dates and records are invented. This is a completed reasoning exercise, not an archival finding or DNA analysis.

The research question

Which Elias Vale appears in an 1891 marriage record: the man born in 1854 or a namesake born in 1859? An online tree favors the second man.

IDRecordSynthetic record content
R01birth entryParents: Joseph Vale and Anna Reed. Mill Street. Invented original-entry abstraction.
R02birth entryParents: Samuel Vale and Ruth Lane. Station Road. Invented original-entry abstraction.
R03marriage entryGroom age 36; father Joseph Vale; occupation joiner; Mill Street. Invented original-entry abstraction.
R04household entryAge 31; wife Ada; Station Road; father Samuel listed in the household. Invented original-entry abstraction.
R05online tree summaryLinks the 1891 groom to the 1859 birth. No independent source beyond a copied marriage index.
R06occupation entryJoiner at Mill Street; father Joseph. Invented original-entry abstraction.

Competing explanations

Candidate A: father, address and occupation converge across separate records. The marriage age is one year younger than the birth record implies; that discrepancy stays in the case.

Candidate B: the online tree adds no independent observation. His father, address and existing household conflict with the marriage entry. Additional explanations are possible, but this packet does not establish them.

A third namesake: the packet is not exhaustive. Two identified candidates do not prove there were only two.

Bounded conclusion

A is better supported within the supplied packet. The next step would be to inspect original images, witnesses and intervening household records, then investigate B’s marital history. This exercise does not claim a completed real-world genealogical proof.

Where DNA would fit

No DNA data are included. A shared paternal lineage would be separate evidence, not an automatic identification of a named ancestor. FamilyTreeDNA’s documentation notes that a shared paternal ancestor may predate a recent genealogical timeframe. See the method references below.

FRAME 17

A restored image gives an archive the ending it always wanted. An archivist finds the detail that makes keeping it impossible.

Original fiction · Treatment & scene · Proposed shot sequence

Read the concept

Original AI-assisted fiction. Unproduced concept; no film, cast, generated footage or festival credit is claimed.

Logline

An archivist preparing a tribute discovers that an AI-restored handshake never happened. Removing it means losing the film’s perfect ending—and finding the person the archive forgot.

The story

Hours before an anniversary screening, Lena compares a restored film with its damaged contact sheet. The restoration has invented a handshake between the venue’s founders. Her producer wants to keep the image: it expresses the story they meant to tell.

A sleeve at the edge of the original leads Lena to a second, wider contact strip. An intact frame shows a film can being passed to the projectionist, identified in its caption as June. The final screening retains the damaged frame and names the person the polished version erased.

EXT. SOUTH BANK RIVER PATH — NIGHT

A quiet stretch of river. Streetlamps catch the wet pavement.

A MAN walks towards a bench. A WOMAN sits at one end,
watching the water. She looks up. He almost keeps walking.

He sits at the other end.

Enough room between them to pretend they are alone.

A boat passes. He watches it. She turns slightly towards him.
He does not turn back.

His hands stay on his knees. His weight shifts a little towards her.

Neither speaks. Neither leaves.

The method
behind the work.

Different subjects. The same obligation to make the reasoning inspectable.

Read the method note ↗
01

Separate the observation from the explanation.

Record what is present before deciding what it means.

02

Look for the evidence that could change the answer.

Competing hypotheses deserve a test, not just a mention.

03

Keep the conclusion within the evidence.

Retain contradictions, missing data and the next decisive check.

04

Leave something another person can inspect.

A source packet, a clear argument, runnable code or a specific creative treatment.
