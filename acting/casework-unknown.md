# Casework: Unknown
## Paranormal mystery / deadpan comedy

**Status:** series-development concept from the supplied acting portfolio. This edition does not present episodes, footage, a commission or a screen credit.

### Premise

A quiet investigator meets a world that gets stranger with every case. He treats the impossible with procedural calm. The comedy comes from the distance between what is happening and how little he allows it to disturb his method.

### Dramatic engine

Each short, self-contained case puts a grounded investigator in contact with an absurd paranormal problem. Observation drives the mystery. Timing carries the humour. The central performance holds the scene together without explaining the joke.

### Performance territory

Deadpan reaction, partner timing, controlled changes of attention, and a gradual shift from curiosity to unease. These are the qualities the concept is designed to explore; the writing is not proof that they have been demonstrated on camera.

### Development direction

A small, contained scene would be the next useful test: one encounter, a clear objective and an escalating obstacle. A later filmed or generated version would require a separate production brief and cleared assets. No production or submission has been carried out in this revision.
