# The Quiet Stage
## Stephan Szwaj — self-directed screen performance

Stephan Szwaj is an independent researcher and self-directed screen performer. His screen practice focuses on classical text, physical comedy and observational character work. He is interested in restrained drama, deadpan comedy and characters whose decisions register before they speak.

### Three strands of practice

**Classical text.** Shakespeare and heightened language as material for breath, rhythm and intention. This is self-directed practice, not a claimed drama-school qualification.

**Physical performance.** An interest in the physical-comedy tradition: timing, stillness, micro-actions and the space between impulse and response.

**Observational character work.** Attention to posture, pauses, changes of intention and the way a person occupies a space. Observation informs fictional choices; it does not establish another person's private motives.

### Performance interests

Psychological drama, deadpan comedy, period work, espionage and paranormal mystery. These are areas of exploration, not credits or externally assessed casting recommendations. Polish and English are listed as working languages in the supplied profile; no recorded accent assessment is presented here.
