# South Bank, After Hours
## Written performance study

**Form:** non-verbal scene; a proposed single take. **Status:** written development material, not a performance recording. Adapted from the scene in the supplied acting portfolio.

### Scene

```text
EXT. SOUTH BANK RIVER PATH — NIGHT

A quiet stretch of river. Streetlamps catch the wet pavement.

A MAN walks towards a bench. A WOMAN sits at one end, watching
the water. She looks up. He almost keeps walking.

He sits at the other end.

Enough room between them to pretend they are alone.

A boat passes. He watches it. She turns slightly towards him.
He does not turn back.

His hands stay on his knees. His weight shifts a little towards her.

She looks at the river again.

Neither speaks. Neither leaves.
```

### Performance choices

The action is a decision to remain. Let the almost-departure happen before the choice to sit. Give the other performer time to affect the scene; do not pre-arrange every pause.

The interest is withheld rather than announced. Hands, weight and gaze carry the changing relationship. Stillness should contain a choice, not become an instruction to suppress all movement.

### Proposed staging

Two performers, one bench and a quiet riverside location. A restrained take, practical light and location sound; no score is proposed. The screen geography has been simplified from the earlier draft so the scene does not depend on an unverified combination of London landmarks. This is a staging proposal, not a record of a shoot or permit.
